Our Story

From Singapore's wellness culture to Delhi's vibrant food scene

A Journey of Wellness & Flavor

Build & Blend was born from 15 years of culinary experience in Singapore's thriving wellness scene. Our founder discovered that true health isn't about restriction—it's about empowerment through choice.

In Singapore, we witnessed how diverse cultures could come together through food, creating something beautiful and nourishing. We saw how customization wasn't just a trend, but a fundamental shift in how people approach their meals and their health.

Bringing this philosophy to Delhi, we recognized a city hungry for authentic, customizable wellness options. A place where students and young professionals needed more than just fast food—they needed fuel for their ambitions, energy for their dreams, and flavors that honored their diverse palates.

Build & Blend is our answer: a space where Mexican, Mediterranean, and Indian culinary traditions meet with respect and authenticity. Where every meal is built to match your unique needs. Where wellness is celebrated, not prescribed.

Our Vision

To become Delhi's most trusted destination for customizable wellness cuisine, where every individual discovers that healthy eating is a celebration of choice, flavor, and cultural diversity—not a compromise.

Our Mission

To craft authentic fusion meals that honor Mexican, Mediterranean, and Indian culinary traditions while empowering every customer to build their perfect plate. We provide fresh, quality ingredients with complete transparency, making wellness accessible, delicious, and deeply personal.

Why We Exist

We exist because wellness shouldn't feel like punishment. For too long, "healthy eating" has been synonymous with bland salads, rigid meal plans, and guilt-laden choices. We reject that narrative. Wellness is self-love in action—it's choosing foods that energize you, flavors that excite you, and meals that align with your unique body and goals. It's about abundance, not deprivation. Celebration, not sacrifice. In a world of one-size-fits-all solutions, we believe in radical customization. Your body is unique. Your preferences are valid. Your wellness journey is yours alone. We're here to support it with every bowl, burrito, and bite. We exist to prove that you can have it all: authentic flavors, premium quality, complete customization, and genuine nourishment. Because you deserve meals that make you feel alive, not just full.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision we make

Respect for Food

We honor the culinary traditions of Mexico, the Mediterranean, and India. Every spice, technique, and flavor combination is researched and crafted with cultural authenticity and deep respect.

Your Wellness

Your health goals are personal and valid. Whether you're vegan, keto, high-protein, or simply exploring, we provide the tools and transparency for you to make informed choices that serve your body. Explore our dietary options.

Real Choice

Customization isn't a gimmick—it's a philosophy. From base to toppings, every element of your meal is your decision. We build the framework; you create the masterpiece. Start building your meal.

Quality Every Time

Fresh ingredients sourced daily. No preservatives. No shortcuts. 15 years of culinary expertise in every dish. Quality isn't negotiable—it's our baseline.

Community First

We're more than a restaurant—we're a gathering place for wellness enthusiasts. Share recipes, attend workshops, connect with like-minded individuals. Thrive together. Join our community.

Cultural Bridge

Food transcends borders. We celebrate the beautiful intersection of Mexican warmth, Mediterranean freshness, and Indian boldness, creating harmony through flavor.
