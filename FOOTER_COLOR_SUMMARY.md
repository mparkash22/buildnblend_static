## Why Medium Gray?

1. **Highest Contrast**: 10.5:1 (best readability)
2. **Neutral Foundation**: Doesn't compete with content
3. **Brand Accents Pop**: Gold and Lime stand out clearly
4. **Professional**: Modern, clean, trustworthy
5. **Versatile**: Works with any content or theme
6. **Timeless**: Won't feel dated
